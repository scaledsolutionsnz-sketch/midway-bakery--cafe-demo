Our story

A proper neighbourhood bakery on Grove Road

Midway Bakery & Cafe sits right on Grove Road in Mayfield, Blenheim — the kind of local spot where the pies are hot, the coffee’s made to order and someone knows your usual. Everything in the cabinet is baked on-site, from the pastry up.

Whether you’re grabbing a pie on the run, sitting in for a flat white and a slice, or picking up bread and treats for home, you’ll get fresh, honest baking and a friendly welcome every time.

  • Locally owned & run in Blenheim
  • Baked on-site fresh every morning
  • Dine in or takeaway — coffee to match
