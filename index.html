Our story

A pie shop first, right on Grove Road

Midway Bakery & Cafe is Johnny’s pie shop in Mayfield, Blenheim. Every morning we bake our pies fresh in our own kitchen, then keep the cabinet stocked with sandwiches, donuts and sweet treats as the day goes on.

We’re on Grove Road, State Highway 1, at the northern end of town, which makes us an easy stop for locals, tradies, truck drivers and anyone heading to or from the Picton ferry. Grab a pie and a coffee to go, or sit out front on a sunny morning.

Good to know

Questions people ask us

Hours, prices, parking and everything else. Can’t see your question? Give us a call.

Where can I get a good pie in Blenheim?

Midway Bakery & Cafe at 76 Grove Road, Mayfield is a pie shop rated 4.8 stars from over 80 Google reviews. We bake 24 flavours fresh every morning, and every pie is $7.80.

What are your opening hours?

We’re open Monday to Saturday, 6:30am to 5:00pm. We’re closed on Sundays.

How much are your pies?

Every pie is $7.80, whichever flavour you choose. Mince, steak, chicken, roasted duck or pork belly, they’re all the same price.

What pie flavours do you have?

There are 24 on our menu: nine steak pies, mince, mince & cheese and potato top, three chicken pies, three breakfast pies, and gourmet flavours like roasted duck & mushroom, salmon, bacon & cheese, slow cooked lamb curry and caramelised pork belly & apple. See the full pie menu.

Which pies are the most popular?

Going by our Google reviews, customers rave about the Benedict Brunch Pie, Roasted Duck & Mushroom, Steak, Jalapeño & Cheese, Salmon, Bacon & Cheese, and the classic Steak & Cheese and Mince & Cheese.

Are your pies baked fresh every day?

Yes. We freshly bake our pies every morning, and we prepare sandwiches, donuts and sweet treats throughout the day.

Do you have breakfast pies?

Yes. The Benedict Brunch Pie, Bacon & Egg and Bacon, Egg & Cheese are all on the menu at $7.80, and we open at 6:30am Monday to Saturday.

What else do you sell besides pies?

Fresh sandwiches, filled rolls, donuts, cheesecake, tiramisu slices and much more throughout the day, plus coffee to go with it.

Do you serve coffee?

Yes. We make barista coffee, so you can grab a coffee with your pie to take away or enjoy here.

Can I eat in, or is it takeaway only?

Both. Most customers grab their pies to take away, and there’s seating out front if you’d like to sit and eat.

Do you deliver?

We don’t offer delivery at the moment. Pop in to 76 Grove Road, or call us on 03 577 5993 if you have a question.

Where do I park?

Parking is free, on the street and in the parking area nearby. It can get busy at peak times, so you may need to walk a few doors down.

Is the shop wheelchair accessible?

Yes, the shop has a wheelchair accessible entrance.

Are you on the way to the Picton ferry?

Yes. We’re on Grove Road, State Highway 1, at the northern end of Blenheim, so we’re an easy stop on the way to or from Picton.
