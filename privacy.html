Privacy Policy

How Midway Bakery & Cafe handles the small amount of information you share with us.

Last updated: 2026. This is placeholder content for the demo and should be reviewed before going live. It is written to align with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

1. Who we are

Midway Bakery & Cafe (“we”, “us”) operates from 76 Grove Road, Mayfield, Blenheim 7201. This policy explains how we collect and use personal information.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you give us when you phone or email, such as your name, phone number and email address.
  • Order details when you place a custom or advance order.
  • Basic, non-identifying website analytics (such as pages viewed), if enabled.

This website has no contact form and does not collect payment information online.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiries and confirm your orders.
  • To provide the products and service you’ve asked for.
  • To improve our menu, service and this website.

4. Sharing your information

We don’t sell your personal information. We only share it where necessary to run our business (for example, a supplier for a custom order) or where required by law.

5. Third-party services

This site uses Google Maps to show our location and links to social media platforms. Those providers have their own privacy policies covering any data they collect.

6. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share and keep it only as long as we need it.

7. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and request corrections. Get in touch and we’ll help.

8. Contact us

For any privacy questions, call 03 577 5993 or email us.
